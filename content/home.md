## Welcome to Cape Fear Foster Parents

Empowering Families. Supporting Children. Strengthening Our Community.

Every Child Deserves a Safe, Loving Home

In the Cape Fear region of North Carolina—spanning New Hanover, Brunswick, Pender, and surrounding counties—hundreds of children are actively in the foster care system each year. Behind every statistic is a child looking for stability, safety, and hope.

At Cape Fear Foster Parents, we believe that strong, well-supported foster families are the key to helping vulnerable children heal and thrive. Whether you are considering becoming a foster parent, currently navigating your foster care journey, or looking for ways to support foster families in our community, you have a place here.

How We Support the Cape Fear Region

We serve as a dedicated bridge for foster families across Southeastern North Carolina, offering comprehensive guidance, education, and community at every step of the journey.

🎓 Comprehensive Training & Education

Navigating trauma-informed care and foster care licensing can feel overwhelming. We provide specialized training sessions, continuing education credits, and practical workshops designed to give foster parents the confidence and tools they need to succeed.

🤝 Peer Support & Mentorship

You don't have to walk this path alone. Connect with experienced foster parents who understand the unique joys and challenges of foster care through our monthly support groups, mentorship programs, and local community events.

📦 Essential Resources & Relief

From practical necessities—like clothing, baby gear, and school supplies provided through our local resource closet—to connecting families with respite care services, we aim to lighten the load for active foster homes.
